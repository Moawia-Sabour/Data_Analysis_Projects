### 1️⃣ EGX Financial Performance
**Problem:** Power BI web queries to Egyptian Exchange pages caused refresh timeouts.
**What I did:** Built a Python ETL (Regex, pandas) that collects quarterly statements into one Excel file, then a 6-page Power BI dashboard with 15+ KPIs in DAX.
**Result:** Page loads cut by **93%** (84 to 6) and refresh time under **5 seconds**; the data covers **196 EGX companies**.

### 2️⃣ Edita Financial Analysis
**Question:** Is Edita growing profitably, and is it sustainable?
**What I did:** Analyzed 20 quarters of income statement, balance sheet, and cash flow data in a 6-page dashboard.
**Findings:** Revenue **EGP 23.92B (+37.4%)**, **17.7%** operating margin, about **54% ROE**.
**Risks:** Q2 2026 operating cash flow turned negative, short-term debt rose to **EGP 1.97B**, and inventory grew **37%** in a year.

### 3️⃣ Global Superstore Sales
**Question:** Where do sales and profit come from, and is growth healthy?
**What I did:** Cleaned and modeled **51,290 orders** with Power Query and DAX, and built an interactive dashboard (KPIs, trends, segments, markets, shipping, products).
**Findings:** **$12.64M** sales, **$1.47M** profit (**11.61%** margin), **Asia Pacific** the top market ($4.0M), **Consumer** the largest segment (51.6%), peaks in November and December.
