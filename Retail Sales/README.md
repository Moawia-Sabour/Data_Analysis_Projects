# E-Commerce & Retail Sales Performance Analysis (2021–2024)

## 📌 Overview
This project provides an in-depth exploratory data analysis (EDA) of retail transaction data spanning from January 2021 through August 2024. Containing over **8,300 transaction records**, the analysis evaluates sales revenue, profitability, product performance, and geographical distribution across the United States.

The objective is to derive actionable business insights regarding revenue drivers, product category margins, and regional market trends to support data-driven business planning.

---

## 📊 Key Metrics Summary
* **Total Transactions Analyzed:** 8,314 orders
* **Total Revenue:** ~$1.93 Million
* **Total Profit:** ~$248 Thousand
* **Total Units Sold:** 31,508 units
* **Time Horizon:** Jan 2021 – Aug 2024

---

## 🔍 Key Findings & Business Insights

### 1. Product Category Analysis
* **Technology:** Generated the highest overall revenue (~$704K) and net profit (~$122K), driven by strong demand for high-value items.
* **Office Supplies:** Accounted for the vast majority of volume sold (19,000+ units) with consistent, high profit margins (~$108K profit).
* **Furniture:** Generated significant revenue (~$622K) but yielded lower relative profitability (~$17.8K), indicating higher operational/shipping costs or aggressive discounting.

### 2. Geographical Performance
* **Top Revenue Drivers:** California (~$390K) and New York (~$246K) lead total sales by a wide margin.
* **Secondary Markets:** Texas ($151K) and Washington ($117K) show strong volume but vary in net profitability.

### 3. Yearly Trends
* **2021–2023 Growth:** Steady increase in annual revenue, peaking in 2023 at ~$609K in total sales and ~$81.7K in profit.

* **Data Transformation:** Data cleansing, handling dates/categorical variables, and creating aggregated reporting sheets.
